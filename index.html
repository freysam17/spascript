Part One

The Script

A narrative journey through chaos, discovery, and transformation.

Act I: Chaos
01

Office Chaos

INT. Corporate Office - Day
"Every day comes with its own chaos"

We open on ARIAN (late 20s-early 30s), visibly stressed at her cluttered desk. Papers everywhere. Deadline notices. Coworkers dump more files on her desk.

Her phone RINGS—home calling. She touches door handles, desk surfaces, shared equipment. Distracted, she touches her face.

Arian
(catching herself)
Not again, Arian. Your hands aren't supposed to touch your face.

More calls. More surfaces. The cycle repeats.

02

False Relief

EXT. City Street - Late Afternoon
"And when you think it's all over..."

Arian walks along an empty street, disheveled, carrying a bag and folder. She wipes sweat from her brow with her file-holding hand.

Her phone PINGS: "Your driver has arrived."

Car door SHUTS. She's in.

03

Commute Chaos

INT./EXT. Ride-Share Car - Continuous
"There's yet more"

Arian sits in the backseat, gazing out the window.

Through the glass: CHAOS. Traffic noise. Market hawkers. Bus stops. Horn blasts. Overwhelming cacophony.

She watches it all pass by, exhausted.

Then—something catches her eye. She straightens up, alert.

(We don't show what she sees. We only see her reaction—a flash of curiosity, recognition.)

She leans forward and GENTLY TAPS the driver's shoulder from behind.

Arian
(intrigued, to herself)
Must be new...
Act II: Transition
04

The Discovery

EXT. Quiet Street - Magic Hour
"Wait... what was that?"

ESTABLISHING SHOT: An empty, serene road.

The car REVERSES into frame, driving backward through the calm.

INT. RIDE-SHARE CAR - CONTINUOUS

Arian replays the moment in her mind, still processing what she glimpsed.

Arian (V.O.)
Wow. This is...

A magical UI button materializes in front of her bearing "MERCY". She taps it.

Act III: Sanctuary
05

The Transformation

INT. Mercy Spa - Golden Hour
"Located in the very heart of Abuja"

TRANSITION: Digital effect transports Arian into the spa.

She appears in a fresh outfit—mid-glam. We see glimpses of her transformation in progress:

  • • Hair styling
  • • Pedicure station
  • • Serene facial treatment

SPEED RAMPS through elegant spaces:

  • • Tranquil reception

  • • Chic salon stations
  • • Relaxation lounge
06

EXT. Abuja Cityscape - Golden Hour

Part Two

Director's Treatment

Visual approach, technical direction, and creative vision.

Concept

This commercial contrasts the relentless chaos of modern urban life with the transformative sanctuary of Mercy Spa. We're selling more than services—we're selling escape, relief, and rediscovery. The spa exists as a real landmark—hidden in plain sight amidst the city's noise—waiting to be discovered.

Visual Approach

Element Chaos Scenes Spa Scenes
Color Cool, desaturated Warm, golden
Camera Handheld, tight Stabilized, spacious
Editing Quick, overlapping Measured, breathing
Sound Layered, overwhelming Soft, ambient
Emotion Stress, exhaustion Peace, transformation

Act I: Chaos

  • Harsh fluorescents, cool blues and grays
  • Handheld, tight frames, claustrophobic
  • Quick cuts, overlapping audio
  • Layered sound: phones, traffic, voices

Act II: Transition

  • Arian's glimpse is the narrative hinge
  • Handheld to stabilized transition
  • Reverse drive symbolizes "rewinding"
  • Gradual warming of color tones

Act III: Sanctuary

  • Warm earth tones, soft golds
  • Fluid gimbal, elegant speed ramps
  • Let moments land and breathe
  • Ambient spa soundscape

The Glimpse

  • We NEVER show what Arian sees
  • Only capture her reaction shift
  • Creates intrigue through implication
  • Subjective discovery experience

Scene 3: The Glimpse Moment

This approach creates intrigue through implication rather than exposition:

Camera Direction
  • Close-up on Arian's face as she looks out window
  • Her eyes track something passing by
  • Subtle shift from tiredness to curiosity
  • This is the pivot—her expression sells the discovery

Narrative Logic: Mercy Spa exists as a real physical landmark in the city. Arian discovers it organically—not through an ad, not through her phone—but by simply seeing it while stuck in traffic.
